# Strategic modernization research notes

## Source reviewed

Hebbia’s 2026 financial-research-platform comparison emphasizes that modern platforms compete on speed of synthesis rather than raw data access. Recurring capabilities include multi-provider integrations, cross-document reasoning, sentence-level citations, workflow automation, natural-language search, comparison grids, portfolio/risk analytics, geographic and supply-chain context, and customizable alerts.

Source: https://www.hebbia.com/resources/financial-research-platforms

## Boundary

Fiscal.ai was not readable in the sandbox because its public site presented a Vercel security checkpoint. No claims are taken from that source.

## Product implications for Market Event Intelligence

The strongest safe upgrades are an evidence graph with sentence-level source links, a company research workspace with durable saved investigations, cross-company comparison matrices, event-to-price reaction analysis, portfolio/watchlist risk views, sector and supply-chain relationship maps, alert rules with cooldowns, and a model/data-quality observability layer. Any claim must remain tied to a dated source or be labeled derived, estimated, or unavailable.
